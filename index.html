Soft-serve hummus

MAKES ~2 CUPS · 90 SEC IN THE PROCESSOR · 30 MIN REST

  • Vegan
  • Sesame
  • No cooking
Hummus (makes about 2 cups)
1 can (15 oz / 425 g) chickpeas drain & rinse process alone until ground15 sec drizzle in slowly with processor running drizzle in slowly with processor running rest at room temp30 min
1 small garlic clove
½ tsp salt
¼ tsp cumin
pinch cayenne
3 Tbs. (45 mL) lemon juice mix
¼ cup (60 mL) water
6 Tbs. (90 mL) tahini whisk together
2 Tbs. (30 mL) olive oil
Texture target
Soft-serve. Light, holds a peak, and drops off the spoon slowly.
Too stiff
Add 1 Tbs. of warm water at a time.
Too soupy
Too much lemon-water went in during the first drizzle. Add more chickpeas or tahini.
Canned beats dried
Rinsed canned chickpeas gave a smoother result than overnight-soaked dried ones, whose skins turned gritty.
Why rest it
Flavours fuse, the garlic loses its raw edge, and the texture firms up to a spreadable density.
Order matters
Grind the dry group first, then drizzle. The drizzling is not optional.

The cayenne is not placed in a step in the original post, so it sits with the other spices here.

Nut-free potato-carrot cheddar sauce

MAKES ~2 CUPS · POURABLE HOT, THICKENS AS IT COOLS

  • Vegan
  • Nut-free
  • For nachos, mac, toasties
Potato-carrot cheddar sauce (makes about 2 cups)
1 cup (150 g) potato peeled, diced boil until very soft, keep the water15 min blend while hot until silky1-2 min thin with extra cooking water if needed
¼ cup (30 g) carrotdiced
⅓ cup (80 mL) olive or sunflower oiladd slowly
¼ cup nutritional yeast
1 Tbs. (15 mL) lemon juice
1 tsp salt
½ tsp each garlic powder, onion powder
¾ cup (180 mL) cooking waterfrom the pot
Boil
Cut the potato into small, even cubes so it cooks in about 15 minutes. A fork should crush it with no resistance.
Blend
Blend while the vegetables are hot, for 1-2 minutes, until completely smooth. Add the oil slowly with the blender running so it emulsifies.
Thin it
Add cooking water a little at a time. The sauce thickens a lot as it cools, so make it slightly thinner than you want.
Reheat
Warm gently in a pot over low heat, stirring, with a splash of water or plant milk to loosen it.
Bake it on
For a bubbly topping, spoon it on and grill for 3-5 minutes.
Storage
Fridge: 4-5 days, airtight. It sets firm and needs reheating with a splash of liquid. Freezer: up to 2 months. Thaw in the fridge, then re-blend or whisk while reheating, because it can turn slightly grainy. Discard if it smells sour or looks slimy, as it is a moist, low-acid sauce.

Stretchy mozzarella-style

MAKES ~1.5 CUPS · BUBBLES, SOFTENS AND STRETCHES IN A HOT OVEN

  • Vegan
  • Cashew
  • Best for pizza
Mozzarella-style cheese (makes about 1.5 cups)
½ cup (70 g) raw cashews soak 2+ h, drain blend until completely smooth1-2 min cook in a pot over medium heat, stirring constantly, until glossy, thick and stretchy3-5 min pour into a lightly oiled mould, chill2 h slice or tear onto pizza, bake at 220-250°C8-12 min
1 cup (240 mL) water
¼ cup (30 g) tapioca starch
2 Tbs. (30 mL) refined coconut oil
1 Tbs. (15 mL) lemon juice
1 Tbs. nutritional yeast
1 tsp salt
Soaking
Soak the cashews in cold water for 2+ hours, or cover with boiling water for 10 minutes if short on time. Drain and rinse before blending.
Blending
Use a high-speed blender for 1-2 minutes. Any grit left over shows in the finished cheese.
Cooking
Stir constantly and scrape the base. The mixture goes from watery to a thick, glossy, stretchy mass quite suddenly, and it catches on the bottom of the pot if left alone.
When to stop
Stop as soon as it pulls away from the sides and stretches when lifted. Overcooking makes it rubbery.
Melting
Tear or slice thin pieces onto the pizza. It softens and bubbles, and a little browning at the edges is normal.
Storage
Fridge: 5 days, airtight or wrapped in baking paper. Freezer: up to 2 months. Thaw overnight in the fridge; it is a little less stretchy afterwards. Use clean utensils, and discard if it smells sour or off, turns slimy, or shows mould or pink or grey patches.

Quick 5-minute pizza cheese

MAKES ~1 CUP · USE RIGHT AWAY ON PIZZA, TOASTIES OR PASTA BAKES

  • Vegan
  • Nut-free
  • Soy
  • No blender
Quick pizza cheese (makes about 1 cup)
1 cup (240 mL) unsweetened soy milk cold whisk everything cold in a small pot heat on medium, whisking, until it thickens and turns stretchy2-3 min spread or spoon straight onto dough, bread or a pasta bake
2 Tbs. tapioca starch
2 Tbs. nutritional yeast
1 Tbs. (15 mL) coconut oil
1 tsp lemon juice
½ tsp salt
½ tsp garlic powder
Cold start
Whisk the tapioca into the cold soy milk first. Added to hot liquid it clumps.
Heating
Use a small whisk and a heavy-based pot. It thickens suddenly at around 60-70°C, so keep the heat at medium.
When to stop
Take it off the heat as soon as it turns glossy and stretchy. It sets quickly as it cools.
Reheat
Whisk in a splash of soy milk over low heat. It melts further in the oven.
Storage
Fridge: best within 3 days, airtight. This is the shortest-lived recipe, with no fat-rich nut base and no culture. Freezer: not recommended, because it separates and turns watery. Make it fresh each time.

Sliceable cheddar block

MAKES ONE SMALL BLOCK · GRATES, AND GOES SOFT AND BUBBLY UNDER A GRILL

  • Vegan
  • Cashew
  • Agar
Cheddar block (makes one small block)
1 cup (140 g) raw cashews soak, drain blend until very smooth cook on medium, stirring constantly, to a gentle bubble and until it holds a ridge4-6 min pour into an oiled mould, cool on the counter, then chill20 min + 2 h unmould, slice or grate; grill at 200-220°C to melt2-4 min
1 cup (240 mL) water
2 Tbs. tapioca starch
1 tsp agar powder
¼ cup nutritional yeast
2 Tbs. (30 mL) refined coconut oil
1 Tbs. (15 mL) lemon juice
1 tsp salt
½ tsp smoked paprika, pinch turmericfor colour
What each does
The agar sets the block firm for slicing. The tapioca helps it soften and stretch when heated.
Agar
Whisk the agar into the blended mixture before heating and bring it to a gentle bubble. Agar only activates after simmering for a minute or two.
Moulding
Use an oiled small bowl, ramekin or silicone mould and smooth the top. To unmould, run a knife around the edge and turn it out.
Melting
Grate or slice thinly and grill, or cover it in a pan with a lid over low heat. It won't flow quite like dairy cheddar.
Storage
Fridge: 5-7 days, wrapped tightly or airtight, away from strong-smelling foods. Freezer: up to 3 months. Thaw in the fridge; slightly crumblier but still melts. Discard if slimy, off-smelling or mouldy.

Cultured, tangy "aged" cheese

MAKES ONE WHEEL · FERMENTED FLAVOUR CLOSEST TO REAL CHEESE

  • Vegan
  • Cashew
  • Cultured
  • Soy (miso)
Cultured cheese (makes one wheel)
1½ cups (210 g) raw cashews soak, drain blend until thick and smooth ferment covered at room temperature (20-25°C) until tangy24-48 h press into a muslin-lined mould and chill12-24 h
½ cup (120 mL) water
1 probiotic capsuleor 2 Tbs. rejuvelac
1 Tbs. white miso
2 Tbs. nutritional yeast
1 tsp salt
Meltable version
After fermenting, blend in 3 Tbs. tapioca starch (about 25 g) and 2 Tbs. (30 mL) coconut oil. Heat gently while stirring until glossy, as in the mozzarella recipe, then chill for 2 hours.
Clean kit
Use a clean jar or bowl and clean utensils, because you are encouraging live cultures to grow.
Fermenting
Press the blend into the container and cover with a clean cloth or loose lid. Taste after 24 hours and stop when it is as tangy as you like.
Good signs
It should smell pleasantly sour and look slightly puffy. Stop and discard if you see mould or pink or grey patches.
Storage
Fridge: 7-10 days for the fermented version, since the acidity helps it keep. The starch-thickened meltable version keeps about 5 days. Freezer: up to 2 months, a little crumblier. Discard if it smells yeasty or unpleasant (rather than tangy), turns slimy, or grows mould.

Note: plant-based cheeses

TIPS FOR BETTER MELT AND FLAVOUR · APPLIES TO ALL FIVE CHEESE RECIPES

Tapioca vs potato starch
Tapioca gives stretch and potato starch gives a creamier melt. Mixing the two works well.
Fat matters
Refined coconut oil gives the firmest set and best melt. Olive oil gives better flavour in sauces.
Flavour boosters
Nutritional yeast, lemon juice or a little apple cider vinegar, white miso, and garlic or onion powder give the cheesy tang.
Cook it
The starch must be heated until thick to activate its stretch. Don't skip that step.
Storage
About 5 days in the fridge in an airtight container. Blocks can be frozen but get slightly crumblier.
