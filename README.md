# Engineer's Recipe Book

A free recipe book laid out like an engineering drawing: each recipe is a grid with the ingredients on the left
and the steps that use them, left to right. 26 recipes, from dips and plant-based cheeses to potjie, rusks and
pancakes.

**Open it:** https://chrispienaarthree.github.io/engineers-recipe-book/

## What it does

- **Portions and batches:** change the number and every amount and pot size scales with it.
- **Menu and shopping list:** tick recipes for a menu; the shopping list adds everything up by shop aisle.
- **Add your own recipes:** type or paste the title, ingredients and method, and the book builds the grid.
  Optional: paste your own Claude API key (from console.anthropic.com) to read photos of recipe cards and
  recipes in any language. The key stays in your browser, and your Anthropic account pays for each recipe read.
- **Backup:** Export saves your recipes, photos and menus to a file; Import loads them on another device.
- **Works offline:** one HTML file. Save `index.html` to a phone, computer or USB stick and open it in any
  modern browser.

Your own recipes and menus are stored in your browser on that device only. Nothing is sent anywhere unless you
use a Claude API key, and then only the recipe you are adding goes to Anthropic.
